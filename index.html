Most local politics runs on guesswork. P&R exists to change that.

P&R Political Consulting Group is a political strategy and research partnership serving candidates, issue campaigns, and local organizations. We are trained in political science and campaigning, and we bring that training to the local level, where it is usually missing.

In practice, that means helping clients plan with evidence: what the research says works, what the data actually show, and where limited time and resources will make the biggest difference.

Practice Areas

Research and strategy for candidates, ballot and issue campaigns, advocacy groups, and local officials, scaled to the size of the challenge.
