Who We Are

P & R Political Consulting Group is a two-person partnership that brings academic research to local, down-ballot campaigns.

Most small campaigns run on guesswork. We help you plan with evidence: what the research says works, what your district's numbers show, and where your time and money will go furthest.

How We Help

Research and planning support for down-ballot campaigns

Campaign Plan

A written plan with your vote goal, timeline, and priorities, grounded in what works in local races.

District Analysis

Past results, turnout, and public data to show where your votes are and where to focus.

Message Research

Issue and opinion research to help you frame a message that connects with local voters.

Voter Contact Plan

Canvassing and GOTV plans based on field research, built for your volunteers to carry out.

Issue Research

Background briefs, questionnaire answers, and debate prep on the issues in your race.

Campaign Review

An outside look at your current plan and materials, with clear, prioritized recommendations.
