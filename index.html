Who We Are

P & R Political Consulting Group is a boutique, low-overhead consulting partnership focused on grassroots, down-ballot races. We bridge academic research with real-world field operations.

Our approach combines data-driven strategy with practical, on-the-ground campaign management. We believe in efficient resource allocation, clear communication, and community-oriented campaigns that resonate with voters.
